I work across recruitment, workforce planning and people development to help organizations build capable, high-performing teams. For me, effective recruitment goes beyond filling vacancies — it is about finding the right talent that aligns with the organization's needs and culture.

Throughout my career in the pharmaceutical industry, I have learned that HR must serve both people and business objectives. A strong employee experience, supported by thoughtful policies and continuous learning, directly improves organizational capability.

My approach is grounded in listening, structured assessment and collaboration with hiring managers and department heads. I believe that when people are set up to succeed, the organization succeeds with them.
